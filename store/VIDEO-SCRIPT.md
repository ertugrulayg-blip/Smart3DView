# Smart3DView — tanıtım videosu seslendirme metni (~2,5 dk)

Köşeli parantez [ ] içindekiler EKRANDA ne gösterileceği (seslendirmeye girmez).
Clipchamp'te her sahnenin metnini ayrı bir "Text to speech" parçası olarak ekle — böylece görüntüyle hizalamak kolay olur.
Önerilen ses: English (US), doğal erkek/kadın ses, hız 1.0 (gerekirse 0.95).

---

**0 — Karşılama** [Logo / başlık ekranı: "Smart3DView for Revit"]
Hello, and welcome to the Smart3DView tutorial. In the next few minutes, I'll show you how to explore your Revit model in a fast, clean 3D window.

**1 — Giriş** [Revit'te kalabalık bir MEP modeli, sonra Smart3DView penceresi açılıyor]
Working in a busy Revit model? Smart3DView opens just the area you need in a fast, clean 3D window. Keep it on a second screen while you model.

**2 — Açma** [Eleman seç → Add-Ins → Smart 3D View; sonra planda dikdörtgen sürükle]
Select a few elements. Forget the classic section box and the chaos inside it — no more hiding, cropping and searching through clutter. Just click Smart 3D View. The area around them opens in seconds. You can also open the section box of a 3D view, or drag a rectangle in a plan view. Linked models are included.

**3 — Gezinme ve tonlar** [Döndür, kaydır, yakınlaştır; 1–7 tuşlarıyla tonları değiştir]
Navigate just like in Revit. Then pick a tone: white, gray, dark, or paper. No materials, no clutter — just clear shapes and solid cuts.

**4 — Renkli mod** [Renkli ton: mavi soğutma, kırmızı yangın, magenta üfleme, yeşil dönüş]
Colored mode keeps the building gray and colors your services. Cooling in blue, fire protection in red, supply air in magenta, and return air in green.

**5 — Çakışma** [C'ye bas → kırmızı/mavi elemanlar; birine tıkla, alt çubukta "clashes with…"]
Press C to find clashes between different services. One side turns red, the other blue, so you see at once what hits what. Click an element to see its partner.

**6 — Ölçü** [D'ye bas; köşede X, ortada daire işareti; Z kilidi; Delete]
Press D to measure in 3D. The cursor snaps to corners, midpoints, edges and pipe centers. Lock the X, Y or Z axis for exact heights and distances. Delete clears everything.

**7 — Panel, etiket, küp** [Yan panel: Mimari/Statik/Mekanik/Elektrik aç-kapat; elemana tıkla → parametreler; T ile boyut etiketi; sağ üstteki küpe tıkla]
The side panel lets you hide whole disciplines, or single categories. Click any element to see its parameters, and select it in Revit with one click. Press T to see sizes as you hover. And use the view cube for exact top, front and side views.

**8 — Kesit kutusu** [B: mavi tutamaçları sürükle; M ile kutuyu kaydır]
Drag the blue handles to resize the section box. The cut updates instantly on your graphics card.

**9 — Yenile ve çoklu pencere** [Revit'te bir kanalı taşı → Smart3DView'de R; ikinci pencere aç]
Changed something in Revit? Press R to reload. Your camera, box and clash results stay as they are. Need another area? Just open a second window.

**10 — Görüntü al** [P → Proje Tarayıcısı → Renderings altında görüntü]
Press P to save a high-resolution picture straight into your model, ready for your sheets.
