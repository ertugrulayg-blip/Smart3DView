### Description
Smart3DView opens the part of your Revit model you're working on in its own fast, GPU-accelerated 3D window — keep it on your second screen and keep working in Revit. Orbit, cut, measure and find clashes without slowing Revit down.

In this video we walk through Smart3DView from start to finish: installation, choosing what to open, navigation, tones, section box editing, clash detection, measuring, tags, Show in Revit, Reload and Take picture.

✅ What Smart3DView does
• Opens your selection, the active 3D section box, or an area you drag in a plan — linked models included
• Detailed color mode: every element type in its own color (trays, ducts, equipment, lighting, sprinklers…)
• Colored mode: the host model and each linked model in its own pastel color, with a legend
• Paper tone: a hand-drawn pencil sketch look for presentations and sheets
• Live section box editing — drag faces or move the whole box; large areas load in slices, Esc cancels
• Clash detection between services in red and blue, with a 5–50 mm tolerance and smart rules (no pipe vs. its own elbow, insulation or connected equipment)
• 3D measuring with AutoCAD-style snapping (corners, midpoints, pipe/duct axes) and X/Y/Z lock
• Tags, Show in Revit, view cube, Reload (R / F5) and multiple windows
• Take picture: about 4K, saved straight into your model under Renderings

✅ Smart3DView neler yapar
• Seçiminizi, 3B görünümün kesit kutusunu ya da planda çizdiğiniz alanı açar — bağlı modeller dahil
• Detaylı renk modu: her eleman türü kendi renginde (tava, kanal, cihaz, aydınlatma, sprinkler…)
• Renkli mod: ana model ve her bağlı model ayrı pastel renkte, açıklamalı
• Kağıt tonu: sunum ve paftalar için elle çizilmiş kalem eskizi görünümü
• Kesit kutusunu canlı düzenleme — yüzleri sürükleyin ya da kutuyu taşıyın; büyük alanlar parça parça okunur, Esc iptal eder
• Tesisatlar arası çakışma kontrolü: kırmızı/mavi, 5–50 mm tolerans, akıllı kurallar (boru–kendi dirseği, izolasyon, bağlı cihaz sayılmaz)
• AutoCAD gibi yakalamalı 3B ölçü (köşe, kenar ortası, boru/kanal ekseni) ve X/Y/Z kilidi
• Etiket, Revit'te göster, görünüm küpü, Yenile (R / F5) ve birden çok pencere
• Görüntü al: yaklaşık 4K, doğrudan modelinize (Renderings) kaydedilir
